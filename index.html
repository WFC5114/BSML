The revival

A modern academy for a life lived in more than one language.

For ninety-five years BSML taught people who had crossed an ocean how to feel at home. Now we are opening again, wider than before, and this time we mean to stay for generations.

Every class at BSML is taught in a language and at a level. You might discuss Aristotle in beginner English, sing in Italian or draw Beacon Hill with instructions in Mandarin. You learn a language by living a full life in it.

Small groups. Named teachers. A school that remembers you.

In every class

Mindfulness, movement, momentum.

These are not extra classes. They are built into everything we teach, as practical skills for a calmer, healthier, steadier life.

Focus and calmMindfulness

Listening fully before replying. Looking slowly at a painting. Staying steady before a presentation.

A large school where you are known by name.

Every member, from seven to seventy, can join one of four Houses: a smaller circle of people who know your name, share dinners and make things together through the year. Joining is optional, and so is leaving.

Our Story

A century of new beginnings.

From a small school for newcomers in 1925 to a modern academy in 2026. These are the moments that shaped BSML.

  1. 1925

    The founding

    BSML opens in Boston to teach English to people newly arrived in America.

    Details to confirm with the owner
  2. 1930s

    Newcomers

    Students arrive by ship from Europe and beyond. The school teaches the language of a new life: work, home and citizenship.

  3. 1972

    A new chapter

    A new generation of leadership takes the school forward and widens its reach around the world.

    Details to confirm with the owner
  4. 1984

    A stone home among the trees

    The school moves to a granite church in Roslindale beside the Arnold Arboretum, its home for more than thirty years.

  5. 1990s

    Every accent welcome

    Accredited English programmes, test preparation and Medical English bring students from dozens of countries.

  6. 2020

    A rest

    After 95 years, the school rested.

  7. 2026

    The revival

    BSML returns as a modern academy of languages, ideas, arts and music, with a new home in Boston.

A letter from our principal

For almost a century, this school welcomed people at one of the bravest moments of their lives: arriving somewhere new and starting again in another language.

We are bringing it back because that work matters more than ever. The new BSML keeps what made it special, small classes and teachers who know your name, and opens it to ideas, art, music and a healthier way of living.

“Since 1925” is not a boast. It is a promise: what you learn here is meant to last a lifetime, and the school means to be here for your children too.

Houses

Somewhere to belong.

In a large, global school, a House is where you are known by name: a smaller circle of people of every age who share dinners, look out for one another and make things together through the year. Houses are optional. You choose one, change it or leave it whenever you like.

Joining

Choose a House at a relaxed welcome evening, or ask us to place you so the Houses stay balanced. Anyone is welcome in any House, and families can share one.

House points

Earned by turning up, practising, helping others and taking part. Points are for fun, never grades.

Head of House

A teacher who knows every member by name, checks in when someone has been away, and hosts the House dinners.

The House Cup

A friendly year of debate, choir, art and movement events, celebrated by all four Houses together at the summer party.

Quietly helpful

Our promises about technology.

We use AI the way a good school uses a good library: to prepare, remember and follow up. People teach.

  • A teacher leads every class.
  • Anything written with AI is labelled and reviewed by a person.
  • Every AI feature can be switched off in your account.
  • AI never pretends to be a person.
  • No AI-made art for students in the School of Arts.
  • Wellness only: we never give medical advice.

Questions

Can I pause my membership?

Yes. Pause for travel or illness for up to three months a year. You keep your House and your progress.

How do I cancel?

In your account, under Membership. Two clicks, no phone call, no questions you have to answer.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Alipay and WeChat Pay. Organisations can pay by invoice.

Is the AI support extra?

No. It is included in every membership, and you can switch it off at any time.
